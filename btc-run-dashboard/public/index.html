      Log

        Prices stream from Hyperliquid's public WebSocket: best bid and ask, 1-minute candles, mark and oracle, and every trade. Your paper venue fills at Hyperliquid's BBO, so the target counts when the side you'd close at, the ask for a short, is at or through it, and closing is a manual press of Close. The bust is checked on candle highs, on the book and on Hyperliquid's mark. The liquidation map and liquidation events come from Coinversa through this site's server, which holds the API key. Nothing runs while monitoring is off; turning it on loads the candles since the position opened (up to 5,000 minutes) and checks them against the bust before going live. Instructions come in three grades: heads-up (no action), get ready, and act now.
